## 📖 About the Project

**eFootball Tournament** is a web application designed to manage and organize competitive eFootball tournaments.

The platform allows users to create accounts, select their preferred football club, and register for tournaments launched by an administrator.

Administrators can create tournaments with different player capacities (4, 8, or 16 players). Once a tournament reaches its maximum capacity, the administrator starts the competition, and players are randomly matched against each other.

Throughout the tournament, administrators manage match results by entering scores, while participants can follow the competition's progress, view match results, and track their performance.

To make the experience more engaging, the platform also includes player statistics, leaderboards, and achievements that encourage competition and create a more exciting gaming atmosphere.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. **Account Creation:** Users create accounts and select their preferred football club.
2. **Tournament Creation:** The administrator launches a tournament with a capacity of 4, 8, or 16 players.
3. **Player Registration:** Users subscribe to the available tournament.
4. **Tournament Start:** Once all available spots are filled, the administrator starts the tournament.
5. **Random Matchmaking:** Players are randomly matched against each other.
6. **Score Submission:** Players play their matches, and the administrator enters the final scores.
7. **Tournament Progression:** Results determine which players advance to the next round.
8. **Final & Winner:** The competition continues until the final match determines the tournament champion.
